\section{SUMMARY}
\small{First-year PhD student at the University of Cambridge's Centre for Human-Inspired AI (CHIA), developing orchestration algorithms for human--AI systems, combining task allocation, skill inference and online learning. My research focuses on pro-human orchestration: optimising how work is distributed while treating human participation as an explicit constraint, not a by-product of maximising output. I developed \resumeLink{https://kostadindev.github.io/skillinfer}{skillinfer}, an open-source Python package, and previously spent three years at Stellar Cyber building human--AI multi-agent security products, demonstrated at RSA and Black Hat.}
